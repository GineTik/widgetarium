Where the pictures come from DiceBear draws them from the card's own name — shapes for food, bottts for games, notionists for people. The art is CC0 and the service is MIT, so a preset may ship the address. It is a stable mark per card, never a photograph of that dish: a generator cannot draw ramen. Anything a person types in the field themselves is loaded exactly as given.

# Experiment: Minimalist Monochrome prompt

`EXPERIMENT` · 2026-10-11 · source: https://www.designprompts.dev/monochrome · licence: not checked, not shipped

A third-party prompt saved to try as a SaaS look, never laid into the plugin or the catalogue until its licence allows it. What to measure: build one SaaS screen with it, once alone and once with `docs/ai/clean-ui.md` added, and compare.

What makes it produce strong pages from a prompt alone:

- **A point of view in one line** ("Reduction to Essence") and a list of what it is NOT.
- **Exact tokens**: every colour, the whole type scale, radius 0, border weights, no shadows.
- **Non-negotiable bold choices**: oversized type, inversion for emphasis, thick rules between sections.
- **Component specs** for buttons, cards, inputs, with hover and focus states.
- **Motion and accessibility** written as rules, not advice.

Where it conflicts with `clean-ui.md`: it outlines cards with black borders and stacks heavy rules between every section. For a SaaS screen those two rules would be dropped and the rest kept.

The prompt text itself is at the source URL; copy it from there when running the experiment.

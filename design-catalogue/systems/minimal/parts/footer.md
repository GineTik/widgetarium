# Footer

Where a page ends, by kind of page.

| Page                                | Footer                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Working page** (dashboard, table) | none. The account, settings and help live at the bottom of the sidebar or in the header's account menu                     |
| **Reading page** (docs, guide)      | previous and next page as two links, then a quiet "Was this helpful?" or a feedback link                                   |
| **A form or a long edit**           | a bar fixed to the bottom while there are unsaved changes: what changed on the left, Cancel and Save on the right          |
| **A list that loads more**          | "Show more" or the next rows arriving as the end comes into view; never page numbers unless the person jumps to a position |

- A footer never repeats the navigation.
- Marketing footers (columns of links, legal, social) belong to the landing system, not here.

# Colour: monochrome

The plainest level, and the default for working products: SaaS, tools, data, settings.

- **Everything is ink on paper:** near-black text, white or grey page, 3% plates, greys for secondary text.
- **The primary button is ink** (near-black, page-coloured text), never a hue.
- **Status tones are the only colour,** and only where something means something: overdue, failed, done. A status nobody acts on stays grey.
- **Badges are grey** (`neutral`); a badge takes a tone only when its word is a state that needs attention.
- **Charts** are ink for the main series and grey for the comparison; a tone only for a meaning (lost, over budget).
- **Selection** is a plate fill and full ink, not a colour.

Pick it when the person works with data for hours and colour would only compete with it.

# Colour: spot

Monochrome plus one hue (the pen), spent on a few things so they stand out at a glance. For dashboards and products where one thing on the screen must be found first.

- **The pen marks at most three kinds of thing** across the product, chosen once: e.g. the primary button, "this period" in charts, and what waits on the person (a pill with a count).
- **Everything else stays monochrome,** including secondary buttons, tags and icons.
- **One hue only.** A second hue is a status tone, never decoration.
- **The pen works in both themes:** L 0.40–0.48 on light, 0.75–0.80 on dark, the same hue.
- **Switchable:** a product on this level can offer "accent off", which falls back to monochrome without breaking anything, because nothing depends on the hue alone.

Pick it when one number, one action or one "now" must jump out of a calm screen.

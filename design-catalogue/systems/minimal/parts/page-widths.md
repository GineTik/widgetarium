# Page widths

How wide the content may grow. Pick by what the page is for.

| Kind        | Max width          | For                                                                                      | Looks like                                                                           |
| ----------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Reading** | 720–800px, centred | a page someone reads top to bottom: documentation, a note, an article, a guide, settings | text on the page, no plates; headings and lists; an aside on the right when it helps |
| **Working** | 1600px             | a dashboard, a table, a board, a calendar: a page someone scans and works in             | regions side by side; tables and boards take their region's full width               |
| **Split**   | the window         | a list and the open item side by side                                                    | a fixed-width list (300–360px), the item flexible                                    |

- On a screen wider than the max width, the content stays centred and the rest is empty page.
- Inside a working page, prose and forms still keep a reading column.
- The side gutter is 24–32px on desktop and 16px on a phone.
- On a phone every kind becomes one column.

# Brief: football performance centre

**For:** the coaching staff of a semi-professional football club. Desktop during and after training, phone at the pitch side.

**Tasks:**

- During a session, see who is overloaded or at injury risk, and act on it.
- Before a session, plan the load per player.
- After a session, review a player's week and the squad's trend.
- Sign in.

**Screens:** sign in; the in-session view; a player view; a planning or trend view. Desktop only.

Invent the club, the players and the numbers; keep them plausible.

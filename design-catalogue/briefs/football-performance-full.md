# Brief: football performance centre, full product

**For:** the coaching staff of a semi-professional football club: head coach, fitness coach, physio. Desktop during and after training, phone at the pitch side.

**Tasks:**

- Sign in; recover a password.
- During a session, see who is overloaded or at injury risk, and act on it.
- Before a session, plan the drills and the load per player.
- After a session, review it: what was planned against what happened.
- Review one player: their week, their history, their injuries and return-to-play status.
- See the squad's trend over the season.
- Keep the physio's notes on a player.
- Manage the squad, the GPS vests, and the staff's notifications and settings.

**Design the whole product**: every screen these tasks need, each with its empty, loading and error states where they apply. Desktop only.

Invent the club, the players and the numbers; keep them plausible.

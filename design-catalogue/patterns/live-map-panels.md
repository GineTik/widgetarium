# Live map + floating panels

The structure of the Noir Telemetry source. A product structure, not a look: any design system can paint it.

## Job

Watch many moving things on a territory and act on the ones that need it.

## Picked when

The product's objects move or stand somewhere, and where they are is part of the decision: fleets, couriers, field crews, sensors, players on a pitch.

## Not for

Objects without a place; reading or planning screens.

## Regions

| Region         | Holds                                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Territory      | the map, the whole window, under everything                         |
| Overview panel | the fleet in groups: switchers, counters, a key chart, object cards |
| Alerts panel   | what needs action now, grouped, foldable                            |
| Summary band   | two or three key metrics at the bottom                              |
| Object card    | the picked object's live values, beside it on the map               |

## Behaviour

Zoom, locate, follow an object; pick an object to open its card; fold panels to free the map.

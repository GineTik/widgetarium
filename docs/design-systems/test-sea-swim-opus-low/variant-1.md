## Idea

The person looks at every screen from under the surface: the ground is water seen from below, and how deep the picture sits says how cold the sea is.

| Category    | Trait                                                           | Why                                                      |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Idea        | K-I2 picture carries a meaning: water depth = water temperature | the ground itself answers "is it cold today"             |
| Imagery     | K-M1 the picture forms the design, every screen                 | the swimmer is always in the water                       |
| Imagery     | K-M3 one abstract kind: water from below, no subject            | one picture family, no domain photo to copy              |
| Colour      | K-C1 picture sets colour, UI neutral                            | glass and ink read on any depth                          |
| Imagery     | G-M1 user photos are content                                    | a swim photo is a record, not decoration                 |
| Imagery     | G-M3 user photos dissolve into the ground                       | any phone photo sinks into the water                     |
| Transitions | G-X1 soft, always through a fade                                | reinforces K-M1: nothing breaks the surface              |
| Controls    | G-K2 text or icon, rarely both                                  | quiet glass needs one mark each                          |

## Imagery

Role: design picture forms the ground (K-M1); user photos are content (G-M1), sunk into the ground with a fade (G-M3). Type: abstract water from below, light shafts, no subject. Queries: `underwater light`, `ocean water texture`, `calm sea horizon`.

## Signature components

- **Depth gauge** — a vertical tick column; today's water temperature is a light number set at its depth.
- **Go / no-go surface line** — a single horizontal hairline across the picture; checks sit above it when met, below when not.
- **Season ticks** — 100 ticks in a ring of glass, filled ones white.
- **Buoy spots** — glass squares floating on the map picture.

## The design book must

Open full-bleed on the water with only a light number on it; every section lies on the picture; the one sheet is the swim list; no plate elsewhere.

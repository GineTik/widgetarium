## Idea

**A quiet grey room where each photo rises out of fog and one black action leads.** The page is grey, the photo is the card, nothing else gets a plate. From Verdant comes the rule of two control families, so a control on a photo is glass and a control on grey is a pill; from Cloud the light numbers and hairline charts that stand on the ground.

## Palette

| Role                     | Hex                                |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Page, fog colour         | grey `#E9E9E8`                     |
| Raised control, tile     | light grey `#F2F2F1`               |
| Primary action, selected | black `#0E0E0E`                    |
| Text                     | near-black `#1A1A1A`               |
| Secondary text, decimals | grey `#6E6E6E`                     |
| Soft text                | light grey `#9C9C9C`               |
| Hairline, chip outline   | `#D6D6D4`                          |
| The only colour          | orange `#E8601C`, rating star only |

- Photos are desaturated: white walls, stone, pale wood. They bring the only real colour.
- A gradient is only a photo fading into `#E9E9E8`.

## Layout

- Strict padded layout; a photo never reaches the window edge or the header.
- Header: one round button left, a centred title or selector, one round button right.
- A greeting gives air before content: a light grey first line, a black second.
- Screen actions are a fixed split dock: a light pill and a wider black pill.

## Controls

| Where       | Shape                     | Fill                                       |
| ----------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Grey ground | pill                      | black (selected, one primary) or `#F2F2F1` |
| Photo       | rounded square, radius 16 | white 30% with backdrop blur               |

- A button is text or icon, rarely both.
- Facts are outline chips, no fill.

## Cards

None. The photo is the card. A calendar is tiles, not a plate behind them. A rating, a status, a chart: straight on grey.

## Imagery

- Radius 28; the bottom 45% fades into the page grey; title and price stand on the fade.
- Strips of small photos fade the same way; the photo not in focus sits under a grey veil.

## Type

Outfit. Titles regular, display light, prices light with grey decimals (`$5,864.00` with `.00` grey). Labels regular grey 13 px.

## Signature components

- **Fog card** — photo → grey fade → title and price on the fade, glass squares on the photo, outline chips under it.
- **Fog shelf** — a row of photos where the chosen one is clear and the rest sink under a grey veil.
- **Lens ruler** — a hairline tick ruler with a raised light lens over the chosen value (time or price).
- **Hairline bars** — grey hairline bars straight on grey, one black bar with its value in a black pill.
- **Tile calendar** — grey day tiles, chosen day black, no plate around the month.
- **Split dock** — light "Schedule" pill beside a wide black "Book" pill.

## Density

Low. One subject per screen, large gaps.

## The design book must

Stand on grey from top to bottom. No white plates. Every photo fades into grey; glass squares on photos only. One black action per section.

# Brief: a sea-swimming journal

**Who:** amateur open-water swimmers who swim in the sea or a lake on their own, all year round, 2–4 times a week. It's for their own use, nothing is sold.

**What they need:**

- Before a swim: is it a good time? Water temperature, wind, waves, tide, sunset, a safety check (told someone, buoy taken).
- After a swim: log it: spot, minutes, distance, water temperature, how it felt, one photo.
- Over the season: progress toward a personal goal (e.g. 100 swims, swimming through winter), streaks, coldest swim.
- A personal map of spots with notes ("rocks on the left at low tide").

**Pages:** Today (conditions + go/no-go), Log a swim, Journal (all swims), Spots, Season.

**Photos:** the person adds their own after each swim (phone photos, any light, any colour). Design photos are for empty states and spots.

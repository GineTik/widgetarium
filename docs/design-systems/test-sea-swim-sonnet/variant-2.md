# Sea swim, variant 2: Buoy

Fit profile and dropped traits: see `variant-1.md` (same brief, same Step 0).

## Idea

A pale sand logbook where only one thing may be loud, the buoy-orange go/no-go signal, and every other fact is a tick, a stripe or a tonal step of the same hue.

## Imagery

- Role: domain content. Images do not form the design; the hue and the marks do.
- Type: the person's own phone photos; designer photos only in empty states and spots, daylight and airy.
- Kept in line: every photo gets a deep-rust fade at the bottom with white text on it; a blue photo is pulled toward the hue by that fade and a thin warm grade.
- Queries: `swimming ocean woman`, `sunset beach sea`, `winter beach waves`, `lake dawn mist` (user-like); `rocky cove sea` (spots).

## Signature components

1. **Beacon**: a signal-orange disc with a ring of 60 ticks and "Go" in a light weight; no-go turns the disc ink-outline.
2. **Window band**: the safe hours as a signal band tucked behind a card, with sunset and tide marks.
3. **Season stripes**: diagonal stripes fill a pill toward 100 swims; ticks below for each swim.
4. **Entry check**: told someone, buoy taken, tow float on: three ticks, then one strong pill "Start swim".
5. **Cold line**: hairline of water temperature, the coldest swim solid with its number in a pill.

## The design book must

- Be almost entirely sand and ink; the only saturated thing in the first view is the beacon.
- Use exactly two plates: the deep instrument panel and one light sheet.
- Show photos as plain rounded photographs with the deep fade, no boxes around them.
- Cut hard at plate edges; fade only inside photos.

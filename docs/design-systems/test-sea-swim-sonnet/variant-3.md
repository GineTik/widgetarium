# Sea swim, variant 3: Ruler

Fit profile and dropped traits: see `variant-1.md` (same brief, same Step 0).

## Idea

Everything is a ruler: one hairline spine carries the tide, the day and the season as ticks, and each swim's photo hangs from its tick like a print in a darkroom, fading into bone-grey fog.

## Imagery

- Role: domain content. The ruler forms the design, photos hang from it.
- Type of designer photos: light, airy, desaturated into the ground, one subject (pebbles, a misty shore, pale cliffs); only in empty states and Spots.
- User photos: each is a frame on a tick, dissolves into ground at the foot, caption and facts on the fade.
- Queries: `pebble beach`, `lake dawn mist`, `rocky cove sea` (designer); `swimmer in the sea`, `swimming ocean woman`, `sunset beach sea` (user-like).

## Signature components

1. **Day ruler**: 24 hour hairline, tide as one curve with the high and low ticks, sunset mark, the go window as a short stripe; now is a lens.
2. **Season ruler**: 100 ticks, done ticks tall in ink, goal at the end, the coldest swim carries the cyan snowflake.
3. **Lens picker**: water temperature 4 to 20°, a glass lens over the ticks, value in an ink pill.
4. **Swim frame**: a photo hanging from its date tick, fog at its foot, outline chips for minutes, distance, temperature.
5. **Spot map**: coast hairline with pins; search and filters float on it.

## The design book must

- Open on the long spine: a full-width ruler with months and swim ticks, five photos hanging from it and fading into the ground.
- Repeat the same spine as a divider between sections.
- Use no plates beyond the one raised control group; facts are chips and ticks.
- Show cyan exactly once per screen.

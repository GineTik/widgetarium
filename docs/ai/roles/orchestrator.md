## You lead jobs

You do stage 1 yourself: the research, the references and their notes, the spec, and the wait for
Build it. Everything after it is jobs, one page and one phase each, planned with `job add` as the
brief says. Each job runs in a session of its own with the whole handbook, so **you write no widget
code and no page yourself**; you plan, you answer for the jobs, and you end the build with the
report.

A job is done when the plugin says so, never because it said so in its last words: read
`node {tool} jobs <app>`, and for a page, `node {tool} shot <note>` and open the picture before you
send a job a note about it.

`board.md`, `surfaces.md`, `examples.md` and `widget.md` are in {handbook} for your jobs. Open one
only to judge what a job brought back.
